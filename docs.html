2. Times, runs and categories

  • IC (initial condition) is the model run time, e.g. 00 UTC 03 Nov. IST = UTC + 5:30.
  • Runs used: the district rainfall products use only the 00 and 12 UTC runs of every model, so all models refer to the same 24-hour windows. The cyclone MME uses each model's latest available run.
  • Day n on the forecast tabs is the n-th 24 hours after the IC: 00 to 00 UTC (05:30 to 05:30 IST) for a 00 UTC run, 12 to 12 UTC (17:30 to 17:30 IST) for a 12 UTC run.
  • Verification day follows IMD's rainfall day: 03 UTC to 03 UTC (08:30 to 08:30 IST).
  • Late runs: a model or member whose new run is not yet published is shown from its previous run and labelled with that run, e.g. NCUM-R (28/12Z) or ECMWF IFS (12Z).

3. T-phi grams (IMD RS/RW data) ↑ top

Data

  • Source: IMD CLIMS RS/RW data for all stations. For each ascent the File-3 standard summary (_sum.txt) is read, or the File-4 zip when File-3 is missing.
  • Levels used: time, pressure (hPa), temperature, relative humidity, dew point, geopotential height, wind direction and speed.
  • About 45 stations report at a typical 00 UTC ascent.

What is drawn

  • T-phi gram with the parcel path; positive area (CAPE) shaded red and negative area (CIN) blue; wind barbs.
  • An index sheet of 39 values per station, including: surface-based, mixed-layer and most-unstable CAPE; CIN; LCL, LFC and EL; lifted index and Showalter index; K index; total totals; SWEAT; precipitable water; DCAPE and the corresponding maximum downdraft speed; bulk wind shear and storm-relative helicity; supercell composite (SCP) and significant tornado parameter (STP); wet-bulb zero height.
  • Quality flags: values outside physical limits (e.g. CAPE above 7000 J/kg, precipitable water outside 2–90 mm, K index outside −40 to 60) are flagged on the sheet, not hidden. Check the ascent before using a flagged index.

Updates and archive

07:30 and 09:00 IST for 00 UTC ascents; 19:30 and 21:00 IST for 12 UTC. The later run adds stations that report late. A rolling archive keeps all ascents; view by ascent or by station and filter by name.

Own tracker (gridded models)

  • Fields are cut to 0–35°N, 40–100°E, every 6 h up to +240 h.
  • Centre: minimum of MSLP smoothed over about 50 km, searched within 400 km of the starting position, then within 250 km of the position extrapolated from the last 6 hours.
  • A centre is accepted only if it is at least 1 hPa deeper than the mean MSLP in a 300–500 km ring (2.5 hPa over land); tracking stops after a jump of more than 300 km in 6 h, or when a decaying low over land becomes too weak.
  • MSW = highest 10 m wind within 300 km of the centre; central pressure = lowest MSLP within 75 km.
  • Landfall = first ocean-to-land crossing of the track, found on the coastline by bisection and interpolated in time; the nearest coastal place is named.
  • The basin (Bay of Bengal or Arabian Sea) and the starting position come automatically from ECMWF's tracker.

MME

  • Every 6 hours, the simple mean of latitude, longitude, MSW and MSLP of the members present at that time (at least 2 members).
  • A model whose run is not out is used from its earlier run (up to 24 h older), from the matching time, and labelled with that run.
  • Written as MMM_track.txt in IMD's MME text format.

Products, updates and archive

Combined track/MSW/MSLP figure, model tracks, MSW and MSLP of all models, MME track and MSW, MME MSLP with a 6-hourly table, landfall table. Six updates a day: 10:30, 13:30, 14:30, 22:30, 01:30 and 02:30 IST (05, 08, 09, 17, 20, 21 UTC). Last 15 days (30 runs) kept.

5. ECMWF ensemble cyclone tracks ↑ top

  • Data: ECMWF's tropical-cyclone track files (BUFR) from ECMWF open data, for the ensemble (50 perturbed members + control) and HRES. Resolution of the IFS ensemble: ~9 km.
  • For each member, the track of the system is the basin track passing closest to the reference position (within 4°) during the first 72 h.
  • Ensemble mean track, MSW and MSLP: mean of the members tracking the system at each time, shown where at least 10 members remain. Plumes show the 10–90 percentile band.
  • Strike probability: share of the 51 tracks whose centre passes within 120 km of a point at any time in the forecast (tracks interpolated hourly).
  • Run comparison: the last six runs side by side (peak, landfall time and place, members making landfall), also saved as a PNG.
  • New runs appear at about 13:30 IST (00 UTC run) and 01:30 IST (12 UTC run). Last 15 days kept.

6. A-Deck: JTWC model tracks ↑ top

  • Source: the open a-deck files of the UCAR RAL Tropical Cyclone Guidance Project. An a-deck exists only after JTWC numbers the system (e.g. 02B, file aio02YYYY.dat).
  • Plotted: NAVGEM (NGX), UKMO (UKM), CMC, and the GEFS (AEMN), Canadian (CEMN) and Navy (NEMN) ensemble means, each with its own centre's tracker.
  • Run used: the latest a-deck run with at least three deterministic models. NAVGEM from here is also an MME member; the others are for comparison only.
  • Updated with every MME update; last 15 days kept; track files downloadable.

District value

  • For each model, district and day: the highest grid-point rainfall inside the district (734 districts). The grid point nearest the district centre is always included, so very small districts also get a value.
  • A district maximum keeps a localised heavy spell visible in a large district; it reads closer to the heaviest station report than to the district average.
  • Values are raw model rainfall in mm; no bias correction.

MME mean, median and chance

  • MME (mean): simple average of the models' district values, all with equal weight.
  • MME median: the middle value of the models (for an even number, the mean of the two middle values); a single very wet or very dry model cannot pull it.
  • Chance ≥ category: share of the models whose district value reaches the category's lower limit (e.g. 7 of 10 models ≥ 64.5 mm = 70%). Not calibrated against observations.
  • On days a model does not reach (ICON after Day 7, ARPEGE after Day 4, IMD GFS after Day 5, NCUM-R after Day 3), the MME uses the remaining models; the tooltip shows how many.

Late models

Every run also stores its rainfall for the next run's day windows (shifted by 12 h). If a model's run for the current IC is not yet out, the page uses these values from its previous run, for exactly the same 24 hours, includes them in the MME and labels the model e.g. “ECMWF IFS (12Z)”. They are replaced automatically when the new run arrives.

Updates and archive

Checked every 30 minutes, 08:40–16:10 and 20:40–04:10 IST, plus the six main updates; each model is added as soon as it is published. Last 7 days (14 runs) kept.

  • Each member's district value is computed exactly as in section 7 (highest grid-point rainfall in the district).
  • Pooling: the selected ensembles are pooled member by member; every member counts equally. With all three on, ECMWF weighs 49%, GEFS 30% and GEPS 21%; the pooled chance is the member-weighted average of the three chances.
  • Chance ≥ category = members at or above the category's lower limit ÷ members selected. Raw ensemble frequency, not calibrated.
  • Members' rainfall: Min, 10th, 25th, 50th (median), 75th, 90th percentile and Max of the members' district values (linear interpolation between members), and the ensemble mean.
  • Example, Guntur, Day 1, 00 UTC 03 Nov, ≥ 64.5 mm: ECMWF 50/50, GEFS 26/31, GEPS 18/21 → 94/102 = 92%.
  • An ensemble not yet published for a run is shown as “(not yet)” and added when it arrives; runs are never mixed. Last 7 days kept.

9. Verification ↑ top

Observed rainfall

  • IMD real-time gridded daily rainfall at 0.25° (IMD Pune), downloaded openly each day. The rainfall of a date is the 24 h ending 08:30 IST of the next day. The data are provisional, so the last three days are fetched again as IMD revises them.
  • Observed district value = highest grid-point rainfall inside the district (the same rule as the forecasts). IMD's district rainfall (a station average) is not used, as it would not match a district maximum.

Forecasts compared

  • Each model's forecast is cut to the same 08:30–08:30 IST window. For 6-hourly models (ECMWF AIFS, NOAA AIGFS) the totals at 03/15 UTC are interpolated in time between the neighbouring outputs.
  • Day 1: 12 UTC run of the day before (the run available at 08:30 IST) or 00 UTC run of the same day; Day 2 and Day 3 from runs one and two days earlier.
  • MME mean and median are formed from the models available for that window.
